# 3D asset attribution

## Casualty characters

The clothed casualty characters are derived from Quaternius' Ultimate Modular Men Pack.
The source models are released under the [CC0 1.0 Universal license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The app uses posed, optimized USDZ derivatives of the Casual Hoodie, Suit, and Worker characters.
Source: [Quaternius Ultimate Modular Men Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html).

## Collision vehicle

The collision vehicle is derived from Car Concept by Eric Chadwick and Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets collection.
The source model and textures are released under the [CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The app uses an optimized USDZ derivative.
Source: [Khronos glTF Sample Assets - Car Concept](https://github.khronos.org/glTF-Assets/).
